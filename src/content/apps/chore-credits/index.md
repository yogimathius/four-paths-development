Chore Credits turns the daily chore argument into a simple agreement your family can actually keep.

Simple, everyday chores count the moment they are marked complete. Bigger jobs, or ones that need a grown-up nearby, wait for a quick parent review, and submitted and approved credits stay clearly separate so nobody is guessing what counts.

It is deliberately not a game. There are no leaderboards, no comparison between siblings, no streak pressure, and no reward animations designed to keep children opening the app. Credits describe effort, not currency; no money moves through the app.
