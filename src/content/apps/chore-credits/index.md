Chore Credits helps a household agree on chores and allowance, then run that agreement day to day. Children claim and complete age-appropriate chores and see their progress; a parent approves work and settles the week.

There are no leaderboards comparing children, no accounts, and no servers. Everything lives on the family's device, with a printable board for the fridge.
