Forge 5x5 turns a short bodyweight workout into a daily habit. Every circuit is five moves, five reps each, five rounds, with no equipment and no sign-up.

Your forge guide, Bram, marks first wins, unlocks, new ranks, and streaks, and otherwise stays out of the way while you move. It works fully offline, with a warm dark theme by default and a light theme if you prefer.
