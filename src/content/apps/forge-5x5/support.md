## Get help

Email [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}?subject=Forge%205x5%20support) with a short description of what happened. It helps to include:

- your device model and Android version,
- the app version (shown in the store listing, or your phone's app info screen),
- what you expected to happen and what happened instead.

Forge 5x5 is made by a small independent studio. Every message is read by a person.

## Privacy and your data

See the [Forge 5x5 privacy policy](/apps/forge-5x5/privacy) for what the app stores and where, and the [data deletion page](/apps/forge-5x5/delete-account) for how to remove it.
