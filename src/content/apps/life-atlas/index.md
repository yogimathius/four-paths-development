Life Atlas is a personal operating system for connecting today's actions to the longer arc of your life.

Instead of flattening everything into one task list, it keeps long-range arcs visible alongside the work happening now. Chapters make archived, current, and emerging horizons easier to understand, and suggestions stay proposals until you deliberately accept or dismiss them.

Every account starts with a focused starter atlas you can reshape into your own.
