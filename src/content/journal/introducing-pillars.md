Sadhana is a progressive journey through Hatha yoga: begin with movement, breath, and awareness, and deepen your practice and understanding at your own pace. The app began as Pillars, and its current focus is this single path of practice.

## Practice without looking at your phone

Sessions are spoken. Each sequence comes with pose illustrations plus alignment and breathing cues, but the voice carries you through, so the phone can rest at the edge of the mat. You can choose a guidance voice, pause and resume, and skip any pose that doesn't feel right for your body that day.

## Start where you are

A short experience check suggests a starting point, whether you've never practiced or you're returning after years away. From there the journey moves through named **stages**. Practices within a stage are positions on the path, not deadlines. There's no calendar telling you that you've fallen behind.

## Progress you can see

Sadhana keeps a history of what you've practiced, so coming back after a gap is easy: it remembers where you were. Light and dark modes and a handful of accent themes keep it calm to look at.

## What we keep, and why

Unlike most of our apps, Sadhana has accounts, because your practice history and progress need to follow you between devices. We keep analytics to fixed choices and counts, never your journal entries or notes, and you can delete your account at any time. Payments for the membership are handled by Google Play; Sadhana never sees card details. The [privacy policy](/apps/pillars/privacy) lists every service involved.

Sadhana offers general wellness education, not medical advice. Work within your own limits.
